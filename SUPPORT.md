## A target cannot be met

Increase the byte limit, reduce dimensions, or try JPEG. PNG preserves transparency and can be larger. Exact dimensions cannot be reduced automatically. Small text may still need review even when file constraints pass.

## Units and sizing

KB is 1,000 bytes; MB is 1,000,000. Fit preserves proportions inside maximum dimensions. Crop fills exact dimensions from the center and can remove edges. Pad keeps the complete photo on an exact canvas. JPEG uses white padding and replaces transparency with white; PNG supports transparent padding.

## Saving and sharing

Use Save to Files for the verified encoded file. Photos and messaging apps may re-encode it. Pixmere cannot control a destination's changes or guarantee website acceptance. Originals are not overwritten. Temporary prepared copies are removed when you prepare again or on the next fresh launch, so save any copy you want to keep.

## Photos permission

Import uses Apple's selected-photo picker. Saving a copy needs add-only Photos access. If denied, enable Add Photos in system Settings or export to Files.
